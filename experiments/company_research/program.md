research Goal: Company / IPO / Sector Analysis
## Company Name
BLS International Services Ltd
NSE: BLS
 
## Objective
Research and analyze a given company, IPO, or sector using an iterative AutoResearch approach. Gather information from multiple sources, extract key insights, and produce a structured investment memo. The goal is to identify business fundamentals, competitive positioning, risks, and form a clear thesis and counter-thesis.

## Scope of Analysis
1. Business Overview
   - What the company does
   - Key products/services
   - Revenue streams

2. Financial Analysis
   - Revenue, growth rates
   - Margins (EBITDA, net)
   - Key trends over time

3. Industry & Competition
   - Market size and structure
   - Key competitors
   - Relative positioning

4. Unit Economics (if available)
   - CAC, LTV
   - Contribution margins
   - Cost structure

5. Growth Drivers
   - Expansion opportunities
   - Strategic initiatives
   - Industry tailwinds

6. Risks
   - Regulatory risks
   - Competitive threats
   - Operational challenges

## Required Outputs (per analysis)
- Business Model Summary
- Key Financial Metrics
- Competitive Landscape Mapping
- Unit Economics (if available)
- Identified Growth Drivers
- Identified Risks
- Bull Case (why the company could outperform)
- Bear Case (why the company could underperform)
- Final Balanced Verdict

## AutoResearch Process
- Search for:
  - Filings (annual reports, IPO docs)
  - Earnings transcripts
  - News and recent developments
  - Competitor information
- Extract:
  - KPIs, financials, and operational data
  - Strategic insights and risks
- Iterate:
  - Identify gaps in understanding
  - Refine queries and gather additional data
- Synthesize:
  - Build structured understanding
  - Form thesis and counter-thesis

## Deliverables
- results/data/raw_sources.json — collected references and sources
- results/data/extracted_data.json — structured extracted insights
- results/analysis/company_summary.json — normalized company profile
- results/report.md — final investment memo

## Input
Provide:
- Company name / IPO name / Sector

## Success Condition
- All sections of analysis completed
- Multi-source research performed
- Clear and structured extraction of KPIs and insights
- Bull and bear cases articulated
- Final memo presents a well-reasoned, balanced conclusion
